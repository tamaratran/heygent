What wiring any option in involves

  1. Drawing. Replace the listening branch of PillView.drawRect_ in overlay.py. It already redraws at 60 fps, and every shape here has a direct NSBezierPath equivalent.
  2. Speaking. Today the capsule hides while the agent talks. Showing it means having _pump_ui in voice_agent.py send state="speaking" with level=self.speaker.level. The speaker already measures that level, but nothing sends it. About 10 lines plus a test.
  3. Idle. Today the capsule disappears 1.2 s after you let go of Fn. A resting capsule means it stays on screen all the time. That's a one-line change, but a real product decision.
  4. Words. Your words already reach voice_agent.py while you talk (input_transcript.added). The white caption card (K) is built but switched off by boss.SHOW_CAPTION = False. The capsule's own text mode (452pt wide, the base for L–O) is built too, but nothing sends it text. The agent's reply arrives as a text stream (turn.delta) that isn't timed to its audio. So showing the agent's words in step with its voice, as this page does, needs an estimate of how far playback has got.
  5. More than a level. Oscilloscope (H) and Spectrum (I) need a richer signal than the one number the overlay gets now: raw samples or frequency bands for every 20 ms block.
